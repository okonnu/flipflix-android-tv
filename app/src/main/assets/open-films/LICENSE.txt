In the discovery hero, Flipflix renders a muted, center-cropped excerpt of up
to 25 seconds with a readability gradient. The Watch trailer action plays the
complete, unmodified trailer using the native fullscreen player. The asset
files themselves are unchanged. These are openly licensed sample replacements,
not Flipflix originals, and do not imply endorsement by the creators or W3C.

Credits are also shown beside the hero and in Local profile > Media credits.
