Discovery playback tests render a muted, center-cropped excerpt of up
to 25 seconds with a readability gradient. In v0.1.21 the Big Buck
Bunny trailer was re-encoded as H.264 Main, yuv420p, 854x480 (from 853x480) for
Android decoder compatibility; its audio was copied without re-encoding.
The other asset files are unchanged. These are openly licensed sample replacements,
not Flipflix originals, and do not imply endorsement by the creators or W3C.

These files are packaged only in debug APKs, never release APKs. They are
injected explicitly by regression tests and are not seeded into Home or Library.
